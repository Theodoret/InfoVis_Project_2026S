GDP and Everyday Activities

PCA Overview

Principal Component Analysis of log GDP and all three activity indicators for the selected year. Countries closer together have a similar economic and lifestyle profile.

Working Hours

Total hours normally worked per week in the main job (overtime included), country average.

TV Time

Average weekday TV watching time (hours), derived from ESS category midpoints.

Social Meetings

How often people meet friends, relatives, or colleagues (1 = never … 7 = every day).
